Drop Peter Obi's photos here (.jpg, .png, .webp, .avif, .gif, .svg).
They will automatically appear in the rotating hero/profile gallery — no code edits needed.

Naming tips:
- Prefix with numbers to control order: 01-..., 02-..., etc.
- The rest of the filename becomes the caption automatically, e.g.
  02-campaign-rally-lagos-2026.jpg -> caption "Campaign Rally Lagos 2026"
- This README.txt is ignored (not an image file).

Restart/rebuild the app after adding files for the change to appear.
